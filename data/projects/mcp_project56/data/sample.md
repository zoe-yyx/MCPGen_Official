# Introduction to Knowledge Graphs

Knowledge graphs represent information as a network of entities and their relationships.

## Key Concepts

- **Nodes**: Represent entities such as people, places, or concepts.
- **Edges**: Represent relationships between entities.
- **Properties**: Additional metadata attached to nodes or edges.

## Applications

Knowledge graphs are widely used in search engines, recommendation systems, and natural language processing.
They enable semantic understanding of data and support complex queries across interconnected information.
